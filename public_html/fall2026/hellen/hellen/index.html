After Vonnegut’s “Shapes of Stories”

in the city of god

by Kathleen Hellen

for Virginia Giuffre, on reading St. Augustine’s City of God, Book I, Chapter 19.

when such as the state is complicit. who acquits the crime without witness. violates the chaste. assaults consent. inflicts shame. who absolves rape. in the city of god. in the city of men. who who who extenuates.

***

Kathleen Hellen is the author of three collections and three poetry chapbooks, most recently young girl in the flower of time from Lily Poetry Review Books. Her debut collection Umberto's Night won The Jean Feldman Poetry Prize from Washington Writers' Publishing House. Hellen's poems have appeared in DMQ Review, Drunken Boat, Pangyrus, Small Orange Journal, and elsewhere.
